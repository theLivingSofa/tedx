Shipping & Collection Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

How you get your order

We do not ship by courier. Orders are handed over in person at the IIM Rohtak campus (the exact place and times are announced by email).

When

Merchandise is made to order after the sale closes. We announce the exact dates, times and place by email to the address you gave at checkout and through TEDxIIMRohtak's official channels.

What to bring

Your Order ID (on your receipt and order page) and your college ID. Someone else can collect for you if they bring your Order ID.

Delays

If production is delayed we will tell you by email with a new date. Uncollected orders are kept until the end of the current academic term.
